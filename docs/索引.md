## Abstract (English)

This is the reading index, the single entry point for anyone arriving at FLINT from
outside. FLINT_CHAN, called FLINT for short, is an in-development language centered on
Windows desktop development, aiming at Zig, C++, and Rust. It splits readers into two
paths and gives an ordered reading line through the outward documents, pointing to the
policy table rather than copying it. It also lists which document wins when two of them
disagree, and what is not in the repository yet. Use it to choose your path and your
order, then follow the links rather than the folder.
